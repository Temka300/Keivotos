# FFmpeg source availability

The Linux portable archive includes the executable supplied by the locked
imageio-ffmpeg package. Its exact version, build options, and license are
reported by `./ffmpeg -version` and `./ffmpeg -L`; the latter is included here
as LICENSE.txt.

FFmpeg sources: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#get-sources
imageio-ffmpeg binary provenance: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg

Keivotos invokes FFmpeg as a separate process to extract local video thumbnails.
Retain this notice and review the exact binary's source availability before
redistribution.
